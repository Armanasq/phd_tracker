## Ingestion and verification policy

The curated records are in [opportunities.json](../data/opportunities.json), and institutionally verified entries with **Go** or **Go with risk** can enter the Action list. Records marked **Check first** remain in the Research queue and evidence ledger. Broad search hits, third-party listings, email-only contradictions and uncertain deadline changes are never automatically promoted.
